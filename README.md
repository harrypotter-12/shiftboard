# ShiftBoard

Housekeeping staff schedules — big, clear, and easy to use.

Staff tap their name, enter a short password, and see their work days. Managers add people and build the schedule.

## Public team link

**https://linenboard.onrender.com**

Everyone on any phone can use that same link. Your computer does not need to stay on.

> Free Render tip: after idle time the site may take ~30–60 seconds to wake up the first time someone opens it.

## How people join

1. Open the link.
2. Tap your name (or **I'm new — make my name**).
3. Enter a short password (4 numbers is fine).
4. Staff see **My days**. Managers also get Team, Make schedule, and People.

Demo accounts on a fresh install:

| Name | Password | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Manager | `1234` | Manager |
| Linda | `0000` | Staff |
| Maria | `0000` | Staff |

## How to use

1. Manager adds people in **People** (or staff create their own name).
2. Manager opens **Make schedule** and adds who works, days, and times.
3. Staff sign in and see **My days** for the week.
4. Managers can use **Team** to view everyone, plus **Save Excel** / **Save PDF**.
5. Forgot password? Staff tap **Forgot password?** — manager sets a new one in **People**.
